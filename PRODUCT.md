# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

**Primary (Phase 1): funders, partners, and institutions** deciding whether to fund or
partner with VLA — foundations, corporates, county and national government, governance/
democracy-sector organisations, and individual donors. They arrive evaluating credibility:
is this real, is it well-run, where does the money go, who is behind it. Often on mobile,
frequently on variable Kenyan bandwidth.

**Secondary: the community and beneficiaries** — families and caregivers, youth community
workers and educators, and children aged 5–18 (including children with disabilities) — who
will later come for events, mobile-library routes, and resources as those collections go live.

## Product Purpose

Virtue Literacy Africa is an initiative of the publisher **Virtue Books** (virtuebooks.co.ke).
It documents the realities of Africa's informal settlements and responds with mobile
libraries, literacy and civic-education events, values-based and accessible books, and AI
learning fellowships (including the Mwangaza early-detection programme). The site's Phase 1
job is to **establish credibility and be fundraising-ready**: communicate the mission, the
five programme pillars, the phased plan, and clear ways to partner or give. Success: a funder
or partner leaves trusting VLA and knowing exactly how to engage.

## Brand Personality

**Warm, credible, grounded.** Human and dignified, not corporate, not childish, not
pity-driven. Voice is plain and specific (professional/organizational, drawn from the concept
note): name the concrete thing being done rather than reaching for emotion or slogan.

## Anti-references

- The retired "homegrown" quirky style on the earlier VLA build: hand-drawn doodles, scribble
  underlines, library stamps/barcodes.
- Generic AI-nonprofit template slop: identical icon-card grids, the big-number hero-metric
  template, stock-everything.
- Cold corporate/SaaS aesthetics: navy-and-gray, clinical, tech-startup feel.
- Pity/poverty framing that presents children as objects of charity rather than protagonists.
- Positive genre reference (adapted, not copied): the Charion nonprofit template's clean,
  photography-led, friendly-modern layout language — compressed and re-fonted, in VLA colours.

## Design Principles

1. **Dignity over pity.** Children and communities appear as capable protagonists, never as
   objects of charity. Imagery and copy reflect this.
2. **Evidence earns trust.** Lead funders with concrete programmes, honest figures, and the
   phased plan. Show what VLA does; do not emote at the reader.
3. **Practice the accessibility we publish.** VLA champions accessible/inclusive books; the
   site itself models that standard rather than just describing it.
4. **Built for Kenyan bandwidth.** Fast, light, legible on mid-range phones and patchy
   connections; imagery optimised, motion cheap and optional.
5. **An initiative with a parent.** Clearly "an initiative of Virtue Books," cross-linked, so
   the lineage (and the publishing of Place Called Kibera) reads as credibility, not confusion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.2 AA**. Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast (no light-gray-on-cream); full keyboard and
screen-reader support; honour `prefers-reduced-motion`; support text scaling and high-contrast
comfortably. Bilingual English/Kiswahili is planned for a later phase. This is on-mission, not
a checkbox: an organisation publishing tactile and high-contrast books should not ship an
inaccessible website.
