# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

**Primary: funders, partners, and institutions** deciding whether to fund or partner with
VLA — foundations, corporates, county and national government, development partners,
research institutions, and individual donors. They arrive evaluating credibility: is this
real, is it well-run, where does the money go, who is behind it. Often on mobile, frequently
on variable bandwidth.

**Secondary: the community and beneficiaries** — families and caregivers, community
facilitators and educators, and children (including children with undiagnosed disabilities)
— who will later come for events, reading spaces, and resources as those collections go live.

## Product Purpose

Virtue Literacy Africa is a standalone organisation advancing literacy for children and youth
in marginalised and displaced communities across Africa, currently working in **Kenya,
Ethiopia, and South Sudan**. It reaches pastoralist communities, refugee populations, and
children in arid and semi-arid areas: places where seasonal migration, displacement, and
education systems designed around fixed populations leave the widest literacy gaps.

The work runs across **five pillars**: literacy programmes, mobile and community learning,
innovation and AI in education (including Mwangaza AI early developmental screening),
community partnerships, and research and evidence. The site's job is to **establish
credibility and be fundraising-ready**: communicate the mission, the five pillars, where the
work happens, and clear ways to partner or give. Success: a funder or partner leaves trusting
VLA and knowing exactly how to engage.

## Brand Personality

**Warm, credible, grounded.** Human and dignified, not corporate, not childish, not
pity-driven. Voice is plain and specific: name the concrete thing being done rather than
reaching for emotion or slogan. Structural language over emotive language — the gap is a
design failure in how schooling is provided, not a deficit in the children.

## Anti-references

- The retired "homegrown" quirky style on the earlier VLA build: hand-drawn doodles, scribble
  underlines, library stamps/barcodes.
- Generic AI-nonprofit template slop: identical icon-card grids, the big-number hero-metric
  template, stock-everything.
- Cold corporate/SaaS aesthetics: navy-and-gray, clinical, tech-startup feel.
- Pity/poverty framing that presents children as objects of charity rather than protagonists.
- Imagery that contradicts the positioning: urban informal-settlement scenes, Western
  bookshops and classrooms, conference rooms of Western adults. All of these shipped at some
  point and were removed in August 2026. See `src/assets/CREDITS.md`.

## Design Principles

1. **Dignity over pity.** Children and communities appear as capable protagonists, never as
   objects of charity. Imagery and copy reflect this.
2. **Evidence earns trust.** Lead funders with concrete programmes and honest description.
   Show what VLA does; do not emote at the reader. Do not publish figures VLA cannot stand
   behind — the site currently carries none, deliberately.
3. **Practice the accessibility we advocate.** VLA screens children for undetected sensory and
   developmental barriers; the site itself should model the standard rather than describe it.
4. **Built for low bandwidth.** Fast, light, legible on mid-range phones and patchy
   connections; imagery optimised, motion cheap and optional.
5. **Standalone.** VLA is not an initiative of another organisation. No parent-brand framing,
   no cross-linked lineage.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.2 AA**. Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast; full keyboard and screen-reader support;
honour `prefers-reduced-motion`; support text scaling and high-contrast comfortably.
Multilingual support is planned for a later phase. This is on-mission, not a checkbox: an
organisation whose third pillar is detecting unnoticed disability should not ship an
inaccessible website.

## Standing constraints

- **Never reintroduce**: Kibera, Mathare, Korogocho, "informal settlements", "Phase 1/2/3",
  *A Place Called Kibera*, AJGAD or any named journal, AI Learning Fellowships, civic
  education as a named pillar, or a book-publishing pillar. All retired August 2026.
- **Photography is interim.** Everything in `src/assets/` is licence-free stock standing in
  until VLA sources photography from its own programme communities. Swap files in place.
- **British spelling** throughout ("programmes", "organisations", "marginalised").
