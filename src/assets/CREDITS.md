## Removed in the August 2026 repositioning

Deleted because they contradicted the new positioning, not merely because they were
un-sourced:

- `kibera-aerial.jpg` — Nairobi settlement with the city skyline. Named in the brief.
- `books.jpg` — a pile of Western business and self-help titles (Carnegie, Buffett,
  *The Daily Stoic*). Had been running as the homepage feature image for a children's
  publishing pillar.
- `fellowship.jpg` — a conference room of mostly white adults at a seminar.
- `mobile-library.jpg` — a US bookstore interior.
- `civic-education.jpg` — young men at golden hour; no longer maps to a pillar.
- `child-reading-book.jpg` — a child reading in a West African waterfront settlement.
  Fine in isolation, but replaced by imagery that matches the drylands framing.
