Why we exist

A child who can read can change the place they come from.

In Nairobi's informal settlements, children grow up with little access to books, few safe places to learn, and almost no say in the communities around them. We work to close those gaps, one settlement at a time, starting in Kibera, Mathare, and Korogocho.

What we do

Five pillars, one ecosystem.

From documenting community life to backing the young people who will lead it next, each pillar carries into the one after it.

i

Documentation, Publishing & Inclusive Books

Documenting settlement life and publishing values-based, civic, and accessible children's books with local illustrators.

ii

Mobile Children's Libraries

Roving units bringing reading directly into communities on a reliable schedule.

iii

Reading, Storytelling & Civic Education Events

Where children read, compete, learn their rights, and are publicly celebrated.

iv

AI Learning Fellowships & Civic Leadership

Young people build AI-powered community projects with mentorship and funding to scale.

v

Inclusive Development & Mwangaza AI

Inclusive books, disability-rights education, and an offline AI tool for caregivers and teachers.

Our story

It begins with the book.

Virtue Literacy Africa grew out of Virtue Books and its 2024 title Place Called Kibera, a documented account of life in one of Africa's most densely populated settlements. Publishing is still the thread through everything we do.
