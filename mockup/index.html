The five pillars

It starts with the book and runs all the way to the young people who will lead these communities next.

01

Documentation, Publishing & Inclusive Books

Documenting settlement realities and publishing values-based, civic, and accessible children's books with local illustrators. It is the thread that runs through every other pillar.

02

Mobile Children's Libraries

Roving units bringing curated, inclusive reading directly into communities on a reliable schedule.

03

Reading, Storytelling & Civic Education Events

Children read, compete, learn their rights, get mentorship, and are publicly celebrated.

04

AI Learning Fellowships & Civic Leadership

Top performers build AI-powered community projects, with mentorship and funding to scale them.

05

Inclusive Development & Mwangaza AI

Inclusive books, disability-rights education, and an offline AI tool for caregivers and teachers.

Our story

It begins with the book

Virtue Literacy Africa grew out of Virtue Books and its 2024 title Place Called Kibera, a documented account of life in one of Africa's most densely populated settlements. Publishing is still the thread through everything we do.
