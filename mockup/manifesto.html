Virtue Literacy Africa · Nairobi

Reading is the first right we can give a child.

An initiative of Virtue Books, putting mobile libraries, books, and a civic voice into Nairobi's informal settlements.

Donate The work
The problem

Potential, capped before a child can read.

  • /Severely limited access to books, reading materials, and enriching content.
  • /Few safe, stimulating spaces for learning and creative expression.
  • /Little exposure to values-based education that builds character and resilience.
  • /Near-total absence of civic education: no sense of rights, or the power to use them.

The five pillars.

From documenting communities to backing the young people who will lead them.

01

Documentation, Publishing & Inclusive Books

Values-based, civic, and accessible titles, documented and made with local illustrators. The thread that runs through every other pillar.

02

Mobile Children's Libraries

Roving units bringing reading into communities on a reliable schedule.

03

Reading, Storytelling & Civic Education Events

Children read, compete, learn their rights, and are publicly celebrated.

04

AI Learning Fellowships & Civic Leadership

Young people build AI-powered community projects, funded to scale.

05

Inclusive Development & Mwangaza AI

Inclusive books and an offline AI tool for caregivers and teachers.
